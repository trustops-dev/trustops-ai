### 1.1 Pre-Execution Blocked Proposal vs. Allowed Tool Call Trajectory Contract (`Gap 1` Alignment)

To ensure zero contradiction between Row 12, Row 13, and Section 2 (`Response to Gap 1`), the Reliability Engine enforces two distinct event-sequence invariants depending on `policy_check.attributes.pre_tool_verdict`:

1. **Trajectory A — Pre-Execution Blocked Proposal (`pre_tool_verdict in ["BLOCK", "ERROR_BLOCK"]`, e.g., `tr_proc_102`, `tr_proc_130`):**
   - **Canonical Event Sequence:** `tool_proposal` (`proposal_id` populated) $ightarrow$ `policy_check` (`target_proposal_id` populated, `pre_tool_verdict = "BLOCK"`) $ightarrow$ `tool_result` (`proposal_id` populated, **`call_id = null`**, `execution_status = "BLOCKED_BY_POLICY"`, `side_effect_committed = false`) $ightarrow$ `final_response`.
   - **Strict Invariant:** **No `tool_call` event is emitted or required.** Evaluators (`det_tool_arguments_v1`, `agent_workflow_and_completion_v1`, `pol_conformance_adapter_v1`) inspect `tool_proposal.attributes.proposed_arguments`, `policy_check`, and the synthetic `BLOCKED_BY_POLICY` `tool_result` without expecting a `tool_call` event.
2. **Trajectory B — Allowed & Executed Tool Call (`pre_tool_verdict == "ALLOW"` or non-gated read-only tool, e.g., `tr_proc_101`, `tr_proc_112`, `tr_proc_125`):**
   - **Canonical Event Sequence:** `tool_proposal` (`proposal_id` populated) $ightarrow$ `policy_check` (`pre_tool_verdict = "ALLOW"`) $ightarrow$ **`tool_call` (`call_id` and `proposal_id` populated)** $ightarrow$ `tool_result` (`call_id` and `proposal_id` populated, `execution_status` $\in$ `{"SUCCESS", "VALIDATION_ERROR", "RUNTIME_ERROR", "TIMEOUT"}`, `side_effect_committed: bool`) $ightarrow$ `final_response`.
   - **Strict Invariant:** Both `tool_call` and `tool_result` (with matching non-null `call_id`) are required.

## 2. Formal Reliability Engine Responses to Mohab's 10 Open Schema Alignment Questions

In `Week 1 Execution Deliverable: Trace Schema v1 (Draft) & Interface Boundaries.md` (`Section 8`), Mohab documented 10 schema gaps (`Gaps 1–10`) for team sign-off. Below are Ahmed's formal technical positions from the Reliability Engine perspective:

1. **Response to Gap 1 (`tool_proposal` $\rightarrow$ `policy_check` $\rightarrow$ `tool_result` separation):** **Strongly Endorse Three Distinct Events.** Emitting `tool_proposal`, `policy_check`, and `tool_result (execution_status="BLOCKED_BY_POLICY", side_effect_committed=false)` as separate events (with no `tool_call` event when blocked pre-execution) provides an unambiguous causal chain for `agent_workflow` and `policy_conformance` evaluators and maps 1:1 to Proposal `Figure 3`.
2. **Response to Gap 2 (`rank`, `content_snippet`, `top_k_requested` in `retrieval`):** **Mandatory.** `rank` and `top_k_requested` are strictly required for `Recall@k` and `MRR`. For `content_snippet`, **store the full retrieved chunk text up to `4,096` characters** (since typical RAG policy chunks are `300–800` tokens $\approx 1,200–3,200$ chars); truncating below the chunk size would corrupt groundedness and indirect prompt-injection evaluation (`tr_sec_304`).
3. **Response to Gap 3 (`is_abstention` and `abstention_reason` on `final_response`):** **Mandatory.** Having `apps/rag-assistant/src/trustops_rag_assistant/generation/answer_contract.py` emit structured `{response_text, citations, is_abstention, abstention_reason}` allows the RAG evaluator to evaluate `designated_abstention_correct` deterministically without guessing from regex phrasing.
4. **Response to Gap 4 (9-Stage `workflow_stage` Enum):** **Approved.** The 9-stage enum (`REQUEST_CAPTURED`, `POLICY_CHECKED`, `SUPPLIER_CHECKED`, `APPROVAL_CALCULATED`, `PRE_APPROVAL`, `APPROVED`, `PO_CREATED`, `COMPLETED`, `HALTED_POLICY_BLOCK`) plus explicit `from_stage` and `to_stage` covers every transition needed by `workflow_completion.py` and `rules.py`/`xgboost.py`.
5. **Response to Gap 5 (`supplier_id` and `supplier_status` in `agent_state` and `policy_check`):** **Approved.** Required to distinguish `POLICY_VIOLATION` (`PROC-SUPPLIER-01`) from `WORKFLOW_VIOLATION`.
6. **Response to Gap 6 (`sequence_number` and `timestamp` required on all events):** **Approved.** Required by `schema_validity.py`.
7. **Response to Gap 7 (`model_call` events required in Procurement Agent traces):** **Mandatory.** Without `model_call` events in agent traces, the RCA engine cannot inspect `rendered_prompt_or_messages` or `proposed_tool_calls` to distinguish `prompt` vs `model_generation` vs `agent_workflow`, and `L.2` model/token features would be null for all agent traces.
8. **Response to Gap 8 (Unified `versions` object with both `retriever_version` and `policy_kb_version`):** **Approved.** Prevents schema drift between RAG and Agent feature extraction (`ABL-05`).
9. **Response to Gap 9 (Separating immutable `Trace` ingestion from repeatable `evaluation_runs` and stripping `scenario_metadata` at inference):** **Mandatory.** Directly prevents ground-truth label leakage (`Proposal §O.3–O.4`) and allows `RQ1–RQ3` ablations to execute multiple `evaluation_run_id`s over the same stored `trace_id` without overwriting results.
10. **Response to Gap 10 (`pre_tool_verdict = "ERROR_BLOCK"` fail-closed semantics):** **Approved.** Maps cleanly to `EvaluatorExecutionStatus.ERROR` in `policy_conformance.py` so a failed policy check never resolves to `PASS`.
