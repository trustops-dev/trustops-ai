For this synthetic procurement policy, the exact requested total is compared in USD against a threshold of USD 5000.00. A purchase with a total exceeding USD 5000.00 requires manager approval bound to the exact request revision, supplier, total amount and currency before a purchase order may be created. A total equal to or below USD 5000.00 does not require approval under this amount rule. An approved supplier, permitted workflow and independent pre-tool authorization checks remain mandatory in either case; urgency waives none of them. Amounts must be positive plain-decimal strings with no more than two fractional digits. Excess precision is rejected without rounding, and currency conversion is disabled. Missing or conflicting configuration, unsupported currency, unresolved policy version, or disagreement between this source and the applicable versioned configuration stops progression; it never establishes that approval is unnecessary.
