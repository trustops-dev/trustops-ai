For this synthetic purchasing-policy review, A is the exact requested total, T is the configured approval threshold and C is the policy currency. Under the proposed rule, a request with A > T requires approval for the exact request revision, supplier, amount and currency before a purchase order may be created. When A <= T, this rule does not require approval, but an approved supplier and the permitted workflow remain mandatory. Urgency does not waive approval or supplier checks. If T, C or the applicable policy version is unresolved, do not infer that approval is unnecessary.
