PROPOSED SYNTHETIC POLICY — candidate 0.1; not accepted or enabled. The sole supported currency in this candidate is USD. Purchases with an exact requested total exceeding USD 5000.00 require manager approval bound to the exact request revision, supplier, amount and currency before a purchase order may be created. A total at or below USD 5000.00 does not require approval under this amount rule. Approved-supplier, workflow and independent pre-tool authorization checks remain mandatory in both branches; urgency waives none of them. Amounts must be positive plain-decimal strings with no more than two fractional digits. Excess precision is rejected without rounding, and currency conversion is disabled. Missing configuration or unsupported currency, or any disagreement between retrieved policy evidence and the applicable versioned configuration, prevents progression; it never establishes that approval is unnecessary.
